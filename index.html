TOOTRIVER

Four animals. One raft. One button each.

Local co-op: up to four on one keyboard. Any seat nobody takes is crewed by a bot that toots when you do.

iPhone: tap Share → "Add to Home Screen" to play without the address bar.

Settings

Low turns off shadows and renders at a lower resolution. Auto starts high and steps down if frames get slow.
